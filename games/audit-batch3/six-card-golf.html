⛳ 6-Card Golf

Waiting…
How to play: Each has 6 cards (2x3). Start by flipping any 2. On turn draw top of Stock or Discard. If from Stock you may replace any card (new card face-up) OR discard it to flip one of your face-down cards. If from Discard you must replace. Matching ranks in a column = 0 for that column. A=1, 2-10=face, J=0, Q=10, K=0. When someone has all 6 up, others get 1 last turn. Lowest wins!
