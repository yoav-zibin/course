Brazilian Truco

A team trick-taking game (seats 1&3 vs 2&4). The face-up card (vira) sets the manilhas: the next rank up is trump, strongest by suit ♦ ♠ ♥ ♣. Win two tricks to take the hand. Call Truco to raise the stakes (1→3→6→9→12); your opponents may accept, run, or raise back. First team to 12 points wins.
