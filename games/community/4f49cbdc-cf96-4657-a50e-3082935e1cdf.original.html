Last Floe

Penguins on melting ice. First everyone places their penguins on 1-fish tiles. Then, on your turn, slide one penguin in a straight line across open tiles — it keeps the fish from the tile it leaves, and that tile melts away. Stranded penguins are stuck; when nobody can move, every penguin still on the ice scores its tile. Most fish wins.
