President

Climb the pile: play cards of a single rank, matching the pile's count and beating its rank (3 is low, 2 is high, jokers highest). Playing the same rank skips the next player; completing a set of four — or playing a 2 — clears the pile. Finish your hand to score; first to 6 points wins the match. Between rounds, the top finishers tax the bottom finishers' best cards.
