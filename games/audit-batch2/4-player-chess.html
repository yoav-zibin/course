4 Player Chess

Four armies on one cross-shaped board, in two teams: seats 1&3 (red & green) against seats 2&4 (blue & yellow). Your partner sits opposite you. Pawns promote to queens at the far edge; a checkmated or stalemated army is eliminated — eliminate both enemy kings' armies to win. If 24 half-moves pass, the team with more material wins. You may also resign your army.
