How to play

Pick up all stones from one of your 6 pits. Sow one by one counterclockwise (toward your store on your end). Skip opponent's store. Last stone in your store = extra turn. Last stone in empty pit on your side = capture that + opposite pit to your store. Game ends when one side is empty; remaining stones go to owner. Most stones wins.
