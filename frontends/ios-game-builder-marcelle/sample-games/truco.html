How to play

weakeststrongest

Usually played in pairs (2 vs 2), or 1 vs 1.

  1. Each card beats the cards before it in the row above. Suits don't matter, except for super cards.
  2. Each hand a turned card (vira) is shown; the next rank up is the super card (manilha), strongest of all: ♣ > ♥ > ♠ > ♦.
  3. Play one card each; the highest wins the round. Best of 3 rounds wins the hand (1 point).
  4. On your turn call TRUCO! to raise the hand to 3. The other side accepts, gives up (you score), or raises: 6, 9, 12.
  5. First side to 12 points wins.
