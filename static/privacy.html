Privacy Policy for BuildPlay

Last updated: October 7, 2026.

BuildPlay (buildplay.fun) is a platform for creating and playing board games online. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you log in, we receive a unique identifier from the login method you choose (Google, phone number, or email address), plus the profile details your provider shares — currently your name and, for Google logins, your email address and profile picture. If you log in with a phone number or email address, we store that number or address so we can send you login codes.
  • Game activity. We store the games you create and the matches you play, including moves, so the site can function.

How we use it

We use this information only to operate BuildPlay: to keep you logged in, to show you your games and matches, and to let you play with others. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except the login and messaging providers needed to verify you (Google, Twilio, or our email provider), and only for that purpose.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account exists. If you delete your account (by merging it into another account), the merged-away account and its data are removed.
