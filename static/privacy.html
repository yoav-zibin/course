Privacy Policy for BuildPlay

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

BuildPlay (buildplay.fun) is a platform for playing board games online. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you log in, we receive a unique identifier from the login method you choose (Google, phone number, or email address), plus the profile details your provider shares — currently your name and, for Google logins, your email address and profile picture. If you log in with a phone number or email address, we store that number or address so we can send you login codes.
  • Game activity. We store the matches you play, including moves, so the site can function.
  • Usage analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (pages visited, approximate location, device type). Google Analytics collects this data under Google's own privacy policy; you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Experiment measurements. We remember a campaign link in this browser when you visit one. We count campaign link visits in daily totals; signup, match-start, and return measurements are associated with your account so the platform can compare experiment outcomes. Account deletion removes the associated assignments and user-level experiment events.
  • Bug reports. When you open the bug reporter, we capture the current app screen for you to review. Sending a manual report shares that screenshot, your description, account identifier and profile name, app and device details, and recent app logs, errors, API request timings, navigation and interaction events. Unexpected client errors automatically send recent diagnostics and your account identity when available, without a screenshot, dialog or action from you. Automatic reports have rate limits and duplicate suppression. Recent diagnostics are kept in a bounded in-memory buffer. We filter credentials from diagnostic text and do not record request bodies or text you type into other forms. Screenshots may show information visible in the app; review the preview before sending.

How we use it

We use this information only to operate BuildPlay: to keep you logged in, to show you available games and your matches, and to let you play with others. Usage analytics help us understand and improve the site. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except the login and messaging providers needed to verify you (Google, Twilio, or our email provider), Google Analytics for usage measurement, and OpenAI Codex for reviewing and repairing submitted bug reports, and only for those purposes. Bug reports are available to platform administrators and the authorized repair workflow, rather than other players.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account exists. If you merge one account into another, the merged-away account remains an alias of the surviving account so existing logins keep working. Your manual and automatic bug reports, their resolution history and feedback are kept as private support records while your account exists and are removed when account deletion completes. To request deletion, log in, open your profile and select Request account deletion. You can undo the request for 24 hours; permanent deletion may take up to another hour.

Token transaction records are kept permanently, including after account or match deletion. They contain user IDs, token amounts and starting balances, the reason and time of each movement, and match IDs when applicable. They do not contain names or login details.

Invitation and recruitment history is kept permanently after account or match deletion. It contains internal account, match, invitation and visitor IDs, inviter relationships, conversion events and timestamps. It does not contain names, contact details, login credentials or request/network data.
