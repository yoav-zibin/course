Privacy Policy for BuildPlay

Last updated: October 21, 2026.

BuildPlay (buildplay.fun) is a platform for creating and playing board games online. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you log in, we receive a unique identifier from the login method you choose (Google, phone number, or email address), plus the profile details your provider shares — currently your name and, for Google logins, your email address and profile picture. If you log in with a phone number or email address, we store that number or address so we can send you login codes.
  • Game activity. We store the games you create and the matches you play, including moves, so the site can function.
  • Builder agent chats. When you use the AI assistant in the game builder, we keep the conversation (your messages, the assistant's replies, and when each was sent) together with the game you were editing, so the site operators can review how games were built.
  • Usage analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (pages visited, approximate location, device type). Google Analytics collects this data under Google's own privacy policy; you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Experiment measurements. We assign signed-in accounts a UI theme and remember a campaign link in this browser when you visit one. We count campaign link visits in daily totals; signup, match-start, and return measurements are associated with your account so the platform can compare experiment outcomes. Account deletion removes the associated assignments and user-level experiment events.

How we use it

We use this information only to operate BuildPlay: to keep you logged in, to show you your games and matches, and to let you play with others. Usage analytics help us understand and improve the site. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except the login and messaging providers needed to verify you (Google, Twilio, or our email provider), Google Analytics for usage measurement, and only for those purposes.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account exists. If you merge one account into another, the merged-away account is kept as an alias of the surviving account (so old logins keep working) and its games and matches move to the survivor.
