    How these metrics are counted

    Accounts match a roster email or a whole first or last name in the display name. Name matches are heuristic; review the matching reasons in the account columns.

    Match totals count started cloud matches, once per person, across all their accounts. Waiting lobbies are shown separately. Device saves appear after they sync.

    All invited players includes direct invites and every later generation, deduplicated by canonical account. Recruited matches count each started match involving those players once, even if several of them played together.

    Average score uses assessed reports only, on a 0–10 scale. A score of zero is included; an unassessed report is excluded. People may share a name match, so their rows must not be added together.
