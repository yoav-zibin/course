Delete your BuildPlay account

BuildPlay (buildplay.fun) is a platform for creating and playing board games online. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information tied to it. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to yoavgamingcoursezibin@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my BuildPlay account”.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to log in, or tell us the phone number or login method your account uses, so we can identify the account. If you play as a guest, include the player code and display name shown in your account menu. Do not send passwords or verification codes.
  3. We verify the request, delete the account, and reply to confirm when it is done. A display name or player code alone does not authorize deletion; we may ask for additional proof that you control the account.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: your name, email address, phone number, profile picture, and the identifiers you use to log in.
  • Matches you own are deleted. In other unfinished matches, your participation ends according to the game's leave rules; this may end the match.
  • Your push notification subscriptions, the invites you created, and the experiment measurements tied to your account.
  • Your in-game token balance, which is part of the account.

What is kept

  • Games you published stay on BuildPlay, so other players can keep playing them. Match records owned by other players may also remain.
  • Bug reports you sent are kept as private support records, as described in the Privacy Policy. If you also want personal information removed from a bug report, say so in your email.
  • Aggregated daily totals (such as visit counts) are not tied to your account and are not affected.

Contact

Questions about deletion: yoavgamingcoursezibin@gmail.com. See also the Privacy Policy.
