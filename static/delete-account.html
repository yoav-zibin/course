Delete your BuildPlay account

BuildPlay (buildplay.fun) is a platform for creating and playing board games online. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information tied to it. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to request deletion

  1. Open BuildPlay in your browser, or open the BuildPlay app, and log in to the account you want to delete. Guest accounts can also request deletion.
  2. Open your profile by selecting your name in the top bar.
  3. Select “Request account deletion”, read the confirmation, then select “Confirm account deletion”. Closing the confirmation or selecting “Keep my account” does not request deletion.

Your profile shows the scheduled deletion deadline. You can keep logging in and using your account during the 24-hour undo window. Deletion never begins before that deadline. After the full 24-hour undo window, permanent deletion may take up to another hour.

How to undo a request

Before the deadline shown in your profile, log in to the same account and select “Undo account deletion” in your profile or the persistent deletion warning. You need an internet connection to request or undo deletion. Logging out, switching accounts, or closing the app does not cancel the request.

After the deadline, the undo window has ended, even if the account has not been permanently removed yet. Once deletion starts, it cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: your name, email address, phone number, profile picture, login identities, and any merged aliases.
  • Every online match you participated in, including completed matches and matches owned by someone else, along with their moves and invitations. These shared matches will also disappear for the other players.
  • Your saved-game library, account-linked experiment records, push notification subscriptions and deliveries, and in-game token balance.
  • Private bug reports and feedback you sent, including their screenshots and diagnostic attachments.

What is kept

  • Platform games stay in the catalog so other players can keep playing them. Deleting your account does not delete published games. Offline saved games and caches stored only on your devices are not remotely erased.
  • Aggregated totals that contain no account information are not affected.
